Doctoral research

Process and quality monitoring in 3D concrete printing. Developed computer vision methods to quantify layer deformation and surface quality, assess buildability and extrudability, and support corrective process action. Concrete 3D printing provides the application context for a broader focus on process observability, quality, and reliability.
